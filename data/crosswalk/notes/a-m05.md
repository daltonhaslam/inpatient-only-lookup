# Pass A, batch m05: codes for a second look

- 69950: I chose Division of the acoustic nerve (008N0ZZ). If the op note describes removing a nerve segment, use Excision (00BN0ZZ). Both group to 143-145 for Meniere disease.
- 56630/56631/56632: partial radical vulvectomy is coded as Excision of the vulva, open. External approach (0UBMXZZ) is possible but I don't think it fits radical depth. Adding groin node resection moves the case from 746-747 to 734-735.
- 56631/56632/56634/56637/56640: I coded the groin node dissection as Resection of inguinal lymphatic only. Some coders also add lower-extremity lymphatic (07TF/07TG) for the femoral nodes. The DRG family doesn't change.
- 57110: CPT doesn't fix the approach. I used the vaginal approach (7). The principal diagnosis is vault prolapse (colpectomy/colpocleisis) rather than vaginal cancer, which is a judgment call about Medicare volume. Both land in 748.
- 57111: I used the open approach because the procedure is radical. The vaginal approach is plausible, and the family is the same (748).
- 57280: Supplement of the vagina with synthetic mesh (0UUG0JZ) for sacrocolpopexy, versus Reposition for a suture-only suspension. Coding Clinic usage on this varies.
- 57305/57307/57308: closing the fistula = Repair of rectum + Repair of vagina. With dx N823 the case groups to MDC 6 major bowel (329-331), not MDC 13. Check whether the vaginal-side repair should be coded at all.
- 57308: the perineal body reconstruction is added as Repair of the perineum muscle (0KQM0ZZ). Confirm it is a distinct objective.
- 57311: Martius flap = Transfer of perineum muscle with subcutaneous tissue (0KXM0Z1). The urethra is reached through a vaginal incision, so I coded the approach as open.
- 57531/58210/58285/58548: I coded the parametria as Excision of uterine supporting structure, not Resection. Para-aortic sampling uses the diagnostic qualifier (X). In v44 these group to 731-733, not 734-735.
- 57540: the typical dx (cervical stump prolapse) is a guess. Cancer or dysplasia gives the same family (746-747).
- 57545: pelvic floor repair = Repair of the perineum muscle (levator). Some coders would use Repair of vagina instead.
- 58150: the typical dx is endometrial cancer (731-733), with fibroids as the alternative (742-743). Volume share between the two is a judgment call.
- 58240: exenteration with cystectomy and ileal conduit groups to 731-733, not 734-735, under v44 hierarchy. Check that this is intended.
- 58520: the principal dx N9971 (iatrogenic perforation) puts the case in MDC 21 (907-909). Trauma (S3763XA) would too, but spontaneous pyometra rupture would not. This is a rare procedure in Medicare.
- 58700: the partial-salpingectomy variant (0UB70ZZ) groups to 744-745. Complete removal groups to 742-743.
