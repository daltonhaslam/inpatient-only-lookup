# Pass A, batch m17: codes for a second look

- 22812: the family depends on how the span is coded. A thoracolumbar span (2-7 thoracic + thoracolumbar + lumbar joint codes) groups 456-458. A pure thoracic 8+ joint fusion (0RG8) groups 523-525 "extensive". Set 1 (456-458) is listed as typical; a reviewer should confirm which span is more common for long anterior deformity fusions.
- 22808/22810/22812: device value. I assumed interbody cages (A) for anterior-column deformity work, with structural autograft (7) as an alternative on 22812 only. The adult degenerative scoliosis dx (M4186, "other forms") versus idiopathic M412- is a judgment call. It does not change the family.
- 22818/22819: PCS has no Resection value for vertebrae, so I used Excision of the vertebra. The fusion that almost always goes with it is a separate CPT and is not included here, so the family is 515-517, not a fusion DRG. The dx (congenital kyphosis Q76415 vs a spina bifida Q05 code as principal) needs confirmation.
- 22830: open Inspection of the vertebral joint groups as an OR procedure (515-517). Confirm that exploration alone is coded only as Inspection.
- 22836/22837/22838: I dropped the open-approach alternatives to keep one approach (thoracoscopic, the usual one). The CPT covers both, and the open variant groups to the same family. For 22838, I assumed the tether aggregates to internal fixation device (4) for Removal/Revision. Verify this against the device aggregation table.
- 22849: I coded Removal + Insertion of internal fixation device in the vertebral joint, with Revision as the alternative. The choice of root operation should be confirmed.
- 22861/22862/22864/22865: the dx for a failed disc prosthesis was set to T84098A (other mechanical complication, other internal joint prosthesis). The lumbar removal codes (22864/22865) group 495-497 rather than a back procedure family.
- 41130/41140: approach. Transoral tongue work is External (X), and total glossectomy is typically open (0). I kept one approach per code.
- 41135/41145/41155/42426: radical neck dissection is coded as Resection of neck lymphatics only. The other structures it removes (IJ vein, SCM, CN XI) could get separate codes under B3.2. The family is already 140-142.
- 41150/41153/41155: floor of mouth has no clear PCS body part value. It is coded through tongue + mandible / neck lymphatics only. 41153 (suprahyoid = partial = Excision of neck lymphatic) groups 143-145, not 140-142.
- 42845: the retromolar trigone and the exact pharynx/tonsil body-part split are judgment calls. The pedicled flap is coded as Transfer of thorax muscle with skin and subcutaneous tissue (pectoralis), and the family is 143-145.
- 42894: in the free-flap variant, Replacement of pharynx includes the removal. The donor site is forearm subcutaneous/fascia per B3.9, and I assumed the left side.
- 42953: low confidence. The usual indication (stricture vs fistula repair) is unclear. Repairing the pharyngeal side with an esophageal dx groups 981-983, so I dropped that set.
- 43107/43108/43112/43113/43117/43118: the conduit is coded as Transfer (stomach/large/small intestine to esophagus, qualifier 5). Some coders omit it, but the family is 326-328 either way. For the distal two-thirds codes (43117/43118), I used Resection lower + Excision middle esophagus.
- 43116: Replacement of esophagus (whole-esophagus body part; there is no upper-esophagus Replacement value) + jejunum harvest. No separate Resection code, because Replacement includes the removal.
