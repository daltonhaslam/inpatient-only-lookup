# Pass B, batch m14: codes for a second look

- 27185: Insertion (implant tether) and Destruction (physeal ablation) group to different families (480-482 vs 498-499). Which technique dominates sets the family. The Medicare principal diagnosis is a guess, because this is a pediatric operation.
- 27187: the approach for a prophylactic IM nail is coded open. A small-incision nail is often reported as percutaneous (0QH636Z), which groups the same. Cement augmentation is not coded separately.
- 27245: the CPT doesn't state an approach, so it is coded open (0QS606Z). Closed reduction with a percutaneous nail (0QS636Z) is common coder practice and groups the same (480-482).
- 27254: whether to add Reposition upper femur depends on whether the femoral head fragment is fixed or excised. Principal diagnosis could be either the posterior wall fracture or the posterior dislocation. Both stay in MDC 8 and 480-482.
- 27258: adductor tenotomy and capsulorrhaphy are treated as part of the reduction, with no separate Division code.
- 27259: the femoral shortening is coded as Excision plus Reposition with fixation of the femoral shaft. This moves the family to 498-499 (Excision drives it). If only Reposition is coded, it falls to 480-482. If the osteotomy is subtrochanteric, upper femur may be argued instead. Low confidence.
- 27280: SI fusion groups to Single Level Spinal Fusion (450/451) in v44. The device value is autologous graft because graft takes precedence over fixation. A separate-site iliac harvest (0QB...ZZ) would be added only if a different incision was used (B3.9).
- 27282: PCS has no symphysis pubis joint value for Fusion. The workaround coded here is Supplement plus Insertion on both pelvic bones. The principal diagnosis (M25359) is a weak fit. Low confidence: needs a coder ruling.
- 27295: hip disarticulation is coded as Detachment at femoral region, not upper leg high. Infection of the amputation stump (T8743) or osteomyelitis would move the case to MDC 21 or MDC 8. Diabetic gangrene stays in 239-241.
- G0414: a symphysis plate spanning both pubic bodies is coded on both pelvic bones (set 3). A single side may be more typical.
- G0415: sacral fixation groups to 518-520, while ilium and SI joint fixation group to 515-517. Which structure is typical drives the family.
- 59120: coded as ectopic POC Resection plus salpingectomy or oophorectomy on the patient. If a reviewer holds that 10T20ZZ alone suffices, the family is unchanged (817-819).
- 59325: no device was coded (0UVC0ZZ) because cerclage tape counts as suture under B6.1b. 0UVC0CZ (extraluminal device) is the alternative and groups the same.
- 59350: coded standalone. On a real claim this sits beside delivery codes and would group to a delivery DRG.
