# Pass B, batch m05: codes for a second look

- 56630-56640: vulva approach is coded open (0) because of the deep radical dissection. External (X) is also valid for vulva. The family is the same either way (746-747 alone, 734-735 with an inguinal lymphatic Resection).
- 57110: typical dx is post-hysterectomy vault prolapse (total colpectomy in the elderly). Vaginal cancer (C52) is the other common reason and lands in the same family (748). Approach is 7 (transvaginal); 0 would also group to 748.
- 57111: coded open for the radical paravaginal dissection. 7 is possible if it was done fully transvaginally; family unchanged.
- 57280: Supplement vagina with synthetic substitute (mesh) vs Reposition vagina (native tissue). Both group to 748.
- 57305/57307/57308: coded as two Repair codes (rectum + vagina). Some coders report only one. N823 sits in MDC 06, so these group to 329-331 either way.
- 57311: Martius flap coded as Transfer perineum muscle with subcutaneous tissue (0KXM0Z1). Urethra and vagina Repair coded open, not 7.
- 57531, 58210, 58548: parametrectomy coded as Excision of uterine supporting structure (separate body part, B3.2a). In the v44 hierarchy, radical hysterectomy for malignancy groups to 731-733, which ranks above 734-735.
- 58285: parametrium not coded, because 0UB4 has no via-natural-opening approach value. Family unchanged.
- 58240: the post-hysterectomy total exenteration set groups to 748 (0UTG ranks above 749 bladder), while the sets with a uterus group to 731-733. Anus resection and pelvic lymphadenectomy are not included.
- 58150: typical dx chosen as endometrial cancer (731-733); the benign fibroid alternative goes to 742-743. Which is more common in Medicare TAH volume is a judgment call.
- 58410 (low): dx choice (prolapse vs pelvic pain); presacral neurectomy coded as Excision rather than Division.
- 58520 (low): non-obstetric uterine rupture dx. Traumatic laceration (S3763XA) chosen; spontaneous rupture (N858) or an intraoperative perforation are alternatives.
- 58540: Repair of uterus used as the fallback root for unification metroplasty.
- 58575: infracolic omentectomy coded as Excision (partial) rather than Resection of omentum.
- 58700: typical dx (chronic salpingitis/hydrosalpinx) is uncertain for Medicare; ectopic alternative groups to MDC 14 817-819.
