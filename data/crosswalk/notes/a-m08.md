# Pass A, batch m08: codes for a second look

- 44626: Hartmann reversal coded as Reposition of descending colon (0DSM0ZZ), which groups to the minor bowel family 344-346. If an Excision of the stomal colon is added, or a coder uses Bypass descending colon to rectum (0D1M0ZP), it moves to major bowel 329-331. The root-operation choice changes the family.
- 44227: same issue for the colostomy-takedown variant (0DSM4ZZ goes to minor bowel). The ileostomy closure with resection (0DBB4ZZ) goes to major bowel.
- 44620: Reposition vs Repair for a stoma closure without resection. The transverse colostomy variant (0DSL0ZZ) goes to minor bowel and the ileum variant goes to major bowel.
- 44660 / 44661: the principal dx choice (N321 vs K5720) moves the case between Major Bladder 653-655 and Major Bowel 329-331. I used N321 for the no-resection repair and diverticulitis when the sigmoid is resected. This needs a coder's call.
- 44211: ileal pouch-anal anastomosis. I treated it as part of the resection. Some coders add Bypass ileum to anus (0D1B4ZQ). The family doesn't change.
- 44212 / 45110: whether to code Resection of anus (0DTQ0ZZ) for the perineal portion. I included it for APR and left it out of the laparoscopic proctocolectomy (often intersphincteric). The family doesn't change.
- 44346: parastomal hernia repair goes to the hernia family 353-355. It becomes major bowel only if stomal bowel is resected, and the CPT doesn't settle which case is typical.
- 44300: a feeding jejunostomy tube (0DHA0UZ) is non-OR, so the case groups medical (digestive malignancy). That is expected. The decompression cecostomy (drainage) groups to minor bowel.
- 44680: there is no exact PCS root for intestinal plication. I used Reposition of small intestine. Low confidence.
- 44700: pelvic exclusion. The mesh sling is coded as Supplement peritoneum (356-358). The omental flap (Transfer omentum to pelvic region) groups to the hernia family 350-352, which is surprising but is what the grouper returns. Low confidence.
- 44820: principal dx for a mesenteric lesion is a guess (D201). The family is driven by the procedure (356-358).
- 44322: this is a pediatric operation (Hirschsprung disease) and rare in Medicare. The biopsy body parts are representative.
- 44899: unlisted code, marked not mappable.
