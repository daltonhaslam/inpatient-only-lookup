# Pass B, batch m06: codes for a second look

- 58750: Root operation. I used Bypass tube-to-same-tube (0U150Z5/0U160Z6, a dedicated qualifier); Repair 0UQ or Excision 0UB of the occluded segment are the other readings. All of them group to 742/743. Principal dx Z310 is valid but rare in Medicare.
- 58752: I used Bypass fallopian tube to uterus (qualifier 9). Reposition is the alternative root. The family is the same either way.
- 58760: I chose Repair over Release for fimbrioplasty. A coder could pick Release if the op note describes only lysis of fimbrial agglutination.
- 58822: CMS's short descriptor says percutaneous. The CPT code is the abdominal-approach (open) drainage, and it was on the inpatient-only list, which fits open. I mapped it as open (0U9_0). Please verify the approach.
- 58943 / 58951 / 58954 / 58958 / 58960: For lymph nodes I used Excision (07BC/07BD). If the op note documents removal of the entire pelvic or aortic chain, use Resection (07TC/07TD); the family does not change. On 58943 the node and peritoneal sampling is coded diagnostic (X). I did not code peritoneal washings (0W9G0ZX), which some coders would add.
- 58958 / 58960: No adnexal or uterine organ code is present, so these group to 749/750 rather than 731-733. That reflects recurrence or second-look surgery after the organs were already removed.
- 21343 / 21344: Frontal bone Reposition with a frontal sinus fracture (S0219XA, from the index entry for frontal sinus fracture) groups to the craniotomy family 025-027. If anterior-table-only fractures are coded S020XXA, it may group differently. Please confirm the dx convention.
- 21145-21147 / 21151 / 21348: I added a Supplement code (bone graft) and the donor-site Excision (B3.9) to Reposition. Some coders treat the interposition graft as integral to the Reposition. That drops the Supplement code but does not change the family (140-142).
- 21154 / 21155 / 21159 / 21160: The Reposition body-part list is my judgment: maxilla, both zygomas and nasal bone, plus frontal bone and both orbits for the monobloc. Orbits were left out of LeFort III because only part of each orbit moves. Q751 groups to MDC 8 (515-517), not head and neck 140-142. Please check whether a coder would sequence the dx differently.
- 21182-21184: I used Replacement with autologous tissue (removal of the tumorous bone is included in Replacement). The alternative is Excision plus Supplement. The family is the same.
- 21188: Low confidence. The CPT covers several non-LeFort midface osteotomies, and the body part and dx (M952) are guesses at the typical case.
- 21247: Body part. I used TMJ Replacement (0RRC/0RRD07Z), by analogy to hemiarthroplasty. Replacement of the mandible (0NRT/0NRV07Z) is the other reading. Typical dx for Medicare is uncertain (ankylosis vs condylar resorption).
- 21268: Dx Q107 (congenital orbital malformation) groups to orbital procedures 113/114. An acquired cause (fibrous dysplasia M8508, or post-traumatic) would move it to MDC 8.
- 20661 / 20664 / 21431: These are non-OR in MS-DRG terms and group medical (551/552 and 157-159), as expected.
