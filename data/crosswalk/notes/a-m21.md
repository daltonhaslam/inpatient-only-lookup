# Pass A, batch m21: codes for a second look

- 50750: no Bypass body part for the calyx itself, so I used Kidney Pelvis as the origin. Some coders may add Excision of the kidney for the lower-pole amputation that exposes the calyx.
- 50760: the root operation depends on the case. Stricture excision with end-to-end anastomosis is Excision of the ureter. Repair of a fresh transection is Repair. Duplex ureter-to-ureter is Bypass. All three land in the same family (659-661).
- 50780 / 50782: Bypass to bladder (new anastomosis, adult stricture) vs Reposition (reflux reimplant). The PCS index points to both. I made Bypass typical for 50780 (adult stricture) and Reposition typical for 50782 (duplex reflux). Same family either way.
- 50783: low confidence. I added Excision of the ureter for excisional tapering as a distinct objective. Folding plication would be Restriction (0TV) instead. Some coders would report Reposition alone. Q622 is a pediatric diagnosis and rare in Medicare.
- 50785: the family changes with the variant. A psoas hitch adds Reposition of the bladder, which moves the case to 653-655. Boari flap alone (Bypass to bladder) stays in 659-661. Check whether the Boari flap should carry its own bladder code.
- 50810: low confidence. Rare operation. With a C679 principal it goes to the neoplasm family (656-658). Exstrophy or other benign diagnoses would go to 659-661.
- 50815 / 50820 / 50825 / 50840 / 50845 / 51590 / 51595 / 51596 / 51597: bowel or appendix harvest is coded separately (Excision of ileum or colon, Resection of appendix). I followed the ileal-conduit convention and B3.9. Confirm reviewers agree.
- 50820 / 50815 / 50825: without a cystectomy, the typical diagnosis is uncertain (neurogenic bladder, radiation cystitis, fistula). With C679 they would group to 656-658 instead of 659-661.
- 50830: low confidence. I used Z436 as principal. A coder might instead sequence the underlying condition.
- 50845: I chose Bypass of bladder to cutaneous with autologous substitute (appendix) plus Resection of the appendix. Without a device value (0T1B0ZD) the family might differ.
- 50930: mapped only ureterovaginal (N821, MDC 13, 746/747). A ureter-colon fistula (Repair ureter + Repair sigmoid) grouped to 981-983 or 749/750 with N821, so I dropped it. The CPT spans both.
- 50940: principal N9989 (postprocedural complication) is my judgment call. Release vs Extirpation of the ligature: I chose Release.
- 51570-51597: I added male variants (Resection of prostate and bilateral seminal vesicles) because most Medicare cystectomies are cystoprostatectomies. They are separate body parts under B3.2. The family is unchanged (653-655).
- 51596: neobladder coded as Replacement of bladder with autologous tissue + Excision of ileum. I did not code ureter-to-neobladder anastomoses separately. Check this.
- 51597: C61 (prostate cancer) as principal would move the case to 715/716. I left it out because the alternative diagnosis would also attach to the female set. Worth a deliberate decision.
- 51820: low confidence. I chose Reposition of the ureters, not Bypass, for the reimplant done with bladder-neck reconstruction. The indication is mainly pediatric (exstrophy).
- 51840 / 51841: Reposition of bladder neck vs urethra for retropubic suspension. Both go to 662-664. Some coders would use Repair or Supplement.
- 51900 / 51920: the fistula diagnosis (N820/N821) puts these cases in MDC 13 (746/747, 742/743), not MDC 11.
- No hip/knee revision codes in this batch, so the grouper fix in progress does not apply.
