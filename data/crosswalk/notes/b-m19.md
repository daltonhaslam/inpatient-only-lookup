# Pass B, batch m19: codes for a second look

- 43620/43621/43622: total gastrectomy reconstruction. I coded esophagojejunal reconstruction as an added Bypass esophagus to jejunum (0D150ZA); a strict B3.1b reading would leave the anastomosis uncoded. Either way the family stays 326-328.
- 43631-43634: distal gastrectomy coded as Excision of stomach. The pylorus has its own body part, so some coders would add Resection of pylorus (0DT70ZZ). Billroth II, Roux and pouch get Bypass stomach to jejunum; Billroth I gets none. No family change.
- 43640/43641: Division of vagus nerve plus Repair of pylorus. The principal dx (bleeding vs uncomplicated duodenal ulcer) is a judgment call.
- 43771: the principal dx decides the family. A band complication (K9509) goes to 326-328; revision for weight-loss failure (E6601) goes to 619-621.
- 43820: malignant outlet obstruction (C250) groups to 423-425; benign obstruction (K311) groups to 326-328.
- 43832: open gastrostomy (Bypass stomach to cutaneous, 0D160Z4) is non-OR in MS-DRG terms, so it groups medical by diagnosis: post-stroke dysphagia gives 056/057, plain dysphagia gives 391/392.
- 43845: duodenal switch coded with 3 codes (sleeve, duodenoileal bypass, ileoileal bypass). Is the common-channel bypass separately reportable? Needs a coder's view.
- 43848: no device-free Revision in PCS, so I gave two alternatives (redo bypass, pouch excision). I dropped a band-removal-only set because with an obesity dx it groups 981-983. Bariatric complication vs obesity dx changes the family. Low confidence.
- 43860/43865: anastomosis revision coded as a new Bypass stomach to jejunum. The marginal-ulcer dx was my pick over stricture.
- 43880: fistula closure coded as Repair of both stomach and transverse colon. If a colon segment is resected, that would be Excision of transverse colon instead.
- 43881: Insertion of stimulator lead into stomach (0DH60MZ). With diabetic gastroparesis (E1143, which sits in MDC 1) it groups 040-042. With idiopathic gastroparesis (K3184) it groups 981-983. That is grouper behavior, not a coding error, but it is worth confirming.
- 43882: the principal dx for a gastric stimulator lead complication is uncertain. I used T85528A (displacement, GI device), which groups 907-909. Low confidence.
- 50010/50045: exploration only, coded as Inspection of kidney. The typical Medicare dx for an exploration-only operation is unclear (I used perinephric abscess and other kidney disorder). Low confidence. The family (659-661) is stable across these dx choices.
