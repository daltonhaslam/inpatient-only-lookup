# Pass B, batch m13: codes for a second look

- 27025: root operation changes the family. Release of upper leg muscle (0KNQ/0KNR, the muscle is what gets freed) with traumatic compartment syndrome groups to 981-983, because v44 has no MDC 21 row for 0KN. Division of fascia (0J8L/0J8M) would group to 907-909. With a nontraumatic dx (alternative) it groups to 500-502.
- 27134, 27137, 27138: Removal plus Replacement groups to 463-465 rather than hip revision 466-468. The tool doesn't parse Appendix E procedure clusters, and v44 lists 0SP90JZ/0SPA0JZ/0SPR0JZ under 403-404 and 463-465. Worth confirming against the real v44 grouper.
- 27132: adding removal of prior femoral hardware (0QP604Z, a separate body part) moves the case from 469-470 to 498-499, because the v44 MDC 8 hierarchy in the tool ranks 498-499 higher. Check that this is real v44 behavior. The coding itself follows B3.2.
- 27120: legacy cup-type acetabuloplasty. I coded it as Excision of acetabulum (reshaping). Supplement or Replacement are possible readings. Low confidence.
- 27146, 27147, 27151, 27156: I coded the reorientation osteotomy as Reposition of acetabulum (0QS4/0QS5). Reposition of pelvic bone (0QS2/0QS3) is the other body-part reading. Both group to the same families.
- 27158: the typical CPT use is exstrophy closure, where the osteotomy alone groups to 981-983 under a bladder dx (expected; the bladder closure codes would be on the same claim). With bilateral hip dislocation it groups to 515-517. Medicare volume is essentially nil. Low confidence.
- 55862, 55865: the operative part is Insertion of radioactive element into prostate, open. I left out the Radiation-section brachytherapy code (DV1) because it doesn't change the DRG.
- 26992, 27070, 27071: the typical dx is pelvic osteomyelitis (M8668). In Medicare, ischial/trochanteric pressure ulcer osteomyelitis is common. An L89 principal dx would move these to MDC 9.
- 27176: CPT doesn't state the approach. I assumed percutaneous in-situ pinning (Insertion, 0QH634Z).
- 27090: I coded it as removal from the whole hip joint (0SP9). A femoral-surface-only prosthesis (hemi) would be 0SPR/0SPS, same family.
