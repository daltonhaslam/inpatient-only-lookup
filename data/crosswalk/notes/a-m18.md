# Pass A, batch m18: codes for a second look

- 43121, 43122, 43123, 43286, 43287, 43288: esophagectomy sets code Resection of each removed segment plus the EG junction, plus Transfer (stomach/intestine to esophagus) for the conduit. The other reading codes the reconstruction as Bypass with autologous tissue, or leaves the EG junction out. All readings group to 326-328, so the family is stable.
- 43124: total resection coded 0DT50ZZ with an upper-esophagus cutaneous bypass for the spit fistula. That is anatomically awkward, because a cervical stump remains. Middle plus lower segment resection is the alternative. The family is the same.
- 43405: Occlusion of the EG junction (0DL40ZZ) is non-OR in v44, so the case groups medical (368-370) even though it is an open operation. These cases usually come with a separately coded esophagostomy or gastrostomy, which would move them to a surgical DRG.
- 43460: I chose Control bleeding, GI tract (0W3P7ZZ) per B3.7. It groups surgical (347-349). Insertion of an intraluminal device into the esophagus (0DH57DZ) groups medical (368-370). The root-operation choice changes the family, so this needs a coder ruling or a Coding Clinic citation.
- 43305, 43312: J860 as the principal diagnosis for an acquired tracheoesophageal fistula sends the case to MDC 4 major chest (163-165). With a postprocedural or malignant fistula as principal, it would go to MDC 6 (326-328).
- 43325: the root for the Thal fundic patch is uncertain. I used Supplement of the EG junction with autologous tissue; Repair is the alternative. The family is unchanged.
- 43313, 43314: congenital atresia repairs are pediatric, so Medicare volume is essentially zero.
- 43360, 43361: I used Z434 (esophagostomy takedown) as the principal diagnosis for delayed reconstruction. If the underlying condition is coded instead (stricture, perforation sequela), the case stays in MDC 6.
- 43496: only the pharynx Replacement set is kept. The esophagus Replacement set paired with hypopharyngeal cancer grouped to 981-983, so I dropped it.
