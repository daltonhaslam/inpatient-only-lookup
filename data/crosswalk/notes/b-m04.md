# Pass B, batch m04: codes for a second look

- 39499: unlisted mediastinum code, left unmapped. No defined operation, so any PCS choice would be a guess.
- 39599: unlisted diaphragm code, left unmapped for the same reason.
- 39561: coded as Replacement of the diaphragm alone, because Replacement includes taking out the excised part. Some coders would report Excision plus Supplement. With sarcoma (C493) as principal dx, Replacement groups to 981-983; with mesothelioma it groups to 163-165.
- 39560 and 39561: the principal dx is uncertain. A diaphragm resection reported as the principal procedure is rare. I used primary soft-tissue sarcoma (C493) as typical and mesothelioma (C450) as the alternative, and the two land in different families.
- 39545: plication coded as Repair. No root operation fits better, but this should be confirmed against Coding Clinic.
- 61624: I made middle meningeal artery embolization for chronic subdural hematoma the typical case. By B4.2 the middle meningeal artery codes to the external carotid. If Coding Clinic assigns it to intracranial artery instead, the family doesn't change (020-022).
- 21615, 21616, 21705: rib removal and scalene division for thoracic outlet coded as Release of the structure freed (B3.13: restraining tissue can be taken out). Coding these as Excision of 1-2 ribs would change the family: G540 with a rib excision probably groups as unrelated. This is the biggest judgment call in the batch.
- 21620, 21627: principal dx is a post-sternotomy organ/space infection (T8143XA, groups to 856-858). If coders lead with osteomyelitis (M86), the family becomes musculoskeletal.
- 21740: Ravitch coded as Reposition of the sternum only. The costal cartilage resection is treated as part of the repositioning, not coded separately.
- 21750: sternal rewiring coded as Reposition with internal fixation. Repair is the other candidate.
- 21603, 60522: mediastinal node dissection coded as Resection of thorax lymphatic. Use Excision if the documentation describes sampling rather than a complete dissection. The family doesn't change.
- 32664: low confidence on dx. For Medicare inpatients I made cardiac sympathetic denervation for VT typical (groups to 361-362). Hyperhidrosis is the alternative and groups to 981-983.
- 32820: low confidence. A post-traumatic chest wall reconstruction can be soft-tissue repair, mesh, or rib fixation (rib fixation has its own CPT codes). Mapped to Repair or Supplement of the chest wall.
- 69535: skin SCC of the ear canal as principal dx groups to 981-983. The parotid cancer alternative groups to 143-145.
- 69554: Resection of glomus jugulare with D447 groups to 981-983 whatever the dx (D356 and C751 were tested). That is the grouper's result, not a coding error.
