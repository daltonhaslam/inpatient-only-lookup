# Pass A, batch m07: codes for a second look

- 21432/21433/21435/21436: LeFort III coded as Reposition of each facial bone fixed (zygoma R/L, nasal, maxilla). The body-part list per case is my judgment. The device value for 21433 (none vs wire) is uncertain. All variants group to 140-142.
- 21436: the bone graft is coded as Supplement maxilla (autologous) plus a separate donor-site Excision (calvarium or iliac crest, per B3.9). Whether Coding Clinic wants a separate Supplement on top of Reposition needs a coder's check. It doesn't change the family.
- 31230: orbital exenteration is coded as Resection of eye (external approach) on top of maxilla Resection. This moves the family from 143-145 (31225) to 140-142, so the eye code drives the payment difference.
- 32659: the pericardial window could be Drainage of pericardial cavity (0W9D4) or Excision of pericardium (02BN4ZZ). Both group to 270-272 with an effusion principal dx. Malignant effusion (I31.31 can't be principal), with lung cancer as principal, groups to 163-165.
- 44005: adhesiolysis is coded as Release of small intestine vs Release of peritoneum. Both group to 335-337.
- 44010: the family depends on the variant. Biopsy (0DB90ZX) goes to 344-346, foreign-body Extirpation to 326-328, exploration only (0DJ00ZZ) to 356-358. The typical-case dx (duodenal cancer) is a guess.
- 44025: low confidence on the typical dx (colon foreign body) and on which variant dominates. All variants go to 344-346.
- 44050: detorsion/reduction is coded as Reposition. Some coders may use Release instead; check whether that changes the family.
- 44055: Ladd = Release duodenum + appendectomy, which groups to 335-337 (adhesiolysis). Congenital dx; essentially no Medicare volume.
- 44144: the mucous fistula is coded as a second Bypass to cutaneous (distal limb). There is no explicit Coding Clinic support, so it needs a coder's check. It doesn't change the family.
- 44145/44146/44147: the rectum is coded as Excision (partial, LAR) vs Resection (complete, coloanal). Typical dx assumed rectal cancer.
- 44151/44156/44157/44158: the continent pouch, J-pouch and ileoanal anastomosis are not coded separately (they're part of the Resection reconstruction). The loop ileostomy is coded as Bypass ileum to cutaneous. A coder may prefer 0D1B0ZQ for the IPAA; it doesn't change the family.
- 44187/44188: the alternative dx is a sacral stage 4 pressure ulcer (diverting stoma for wound care). It groups to 981-983 (unrelated OR procedure), which is the correct grouper result, not an error.
