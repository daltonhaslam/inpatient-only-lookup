# Pass A, batch m03: codes for a second look

- 32503/32504: Pancoast resection coded as upper lobectomy + Excision of ribs (1-2 vs 3+). Some cases are sublobar or also take vertebra/subclavian vessel; reconstruction assumed prosthetic mesh (Supplement chest wall). Replacement of ribs is the other reading if the rib segments are rebuilt.
- 32540: empyema sac removal coded as Excision of pleura; Extraction (decortication) is the competing root operation. Both group to 163-165.
- 32650: talc poudrage = Introduction 3E0L4GC, which groups to 166-168 here (not medical, as I expected). Mechanical abrasion = Destruction of pleura, 163-165. The typical principal dx follows the "code first underlying neoplasm" note on J91.0, so lung cancer is principal.
- 32653: Extirpation of matter from pleural cavity is non-OR, so the stay groups medical (177-179 with empyema). That's the correctly coded answer, not a mapping error.
- 32672: bilateral thoracoscopic LVRS uses the bilateral body-part value (0BBM4ZZ), which MS-DRG v44 treats as non-OR (groups to COPD 190-192). The open bilateral code 0BBM0ZZ groups to 163-165. That's a grouper quirk worth a coder's eye. Coding right and left separately would group surgical but goes against the bilateral body-part guideline.
- 32815: bronchial stump level varies (main vs lobar bronchus). Muscle-flap buttress is a separate CPT, so it isn't included.
- 32940: obsolete extraperiosteal collapse/plombage procedure; Release of lung is a best fit with no clear precedent. The packing material isn't coded. Low confidence.
- 32997: whole-lung lavage = Irrigation of respiratory tract via artificial opening (double-lumen tube, approach 7). It groups medical (196-198). 3E1F88Z if done through a bronchoscope.
- 32673: thymoma (C37) puts the case in MDC 17 (826-828). Myasthenia gravis puts it in 040-042.
- 38562/38564: the "for staging" sampling is coded with the diagnostic qualifier X (B3.4a). With X, 38562 groups in-MDC (749/750 for endometrial, 715/716 for prostate). Without X it groups 987-989. 38564 (aortic only) groups 987-989 either way with a gynecologic primary. Low confidence: the X-vs-Z choice drives the family.
- 38765: our plain label says "superficial" only, but this CPT is the groin dissection done in continuity with the iliac/obturator (pelvic) nodes. It's mapped as inguinal + pelvis Resection. The label may need updating.
- 38780: mapped as aortic + pelvis Resection (the CPT spans pelvic, aortic and renal nodes). The principal dx is a guess for a rare Medicare case (secondary nodal disease vs testicular primary).
- 38724: C770 groups 820-822 (MDC 17). A head/neck primary (e.g. C01) would group 140-142. Thyroid cancer would group 628-630. The principal dx mix for a standalone neck dissection is uncertain.
- 39000/39010: the objective varies (drainage vs exploration vs biopsy). Inspection-only groups higher (163-165) than Drainage (166-168).
