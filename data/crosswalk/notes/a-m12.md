# Pass A, batch m12: codes for a second look

- 47785 — one vs two hepatic-duct Bypass codes depends on whether right and left ducts are anastomosed separately or as a confluence (common hepatic duct 0F170ZB would also be defensible for a hilar anastomosis).
- 47800 — Excision of the duct segment vs Repair (0FQ90ZZ) depends on whether tissue is removed before the end-to-end anastomosis; same DRG family either way.
- 47801 — open stent placement coded as Dilation with intraluminal device; Insertion is not available for this body part/device, but confirm.
- 47900 — typical PDx K91.89 (postprocedural bile duct injury, sequenced first per complication guidelines) groups to 981-983; S36.13XA (trauma) groups 408-410. Confirm which PDx reviewers want as typical.
- 48000 — peripancreatic drainage coded to pancreas body part; a coder could pick peritoneal cavity/retroperitoneum (0W9G00Z/0W9H00Z), which may change the family.
- 48001 — four codes (pancreas drains, cholecystostomy, gastrostomy, feeding jejunostomy); gastrostomy coded as Drainage of stomach (decompression). If coded as feeding device (0DH60UZ) family is unchanged.
- 48105 — Excision vs Extirpation for necrosectomy; same family.
- 48145 — remnant pancreaticojejunostomy not coded separately (consistent with Whipple practice), so PCS is identical to 48140.
- 48148 — Resection of ampulla chosen (whole ampulla removed); Excision 0FBC0ZZ if partial.
- 48150/48152 — classic Whipple coded as Excision of stomach (antrum); some coders use Resection of pylorus 0DT70ZZ instead.
- 48155 — en bloc organ set (duodenum, gallbladder, distal CBD, spleen) varies by surgeon; isolated Resection of pancreas offered as second set.
- 48500 — marsupialization coded as Drainage; PCS index also permits Excision when cyst wall is removed.
- 48520 — cystgastrostomy (most common variant) has no Bypass pancreas-to-stomach value, coded as Drainage of pancreas; cystduodenostomy as Bypass pancreas to duodenum.
- 48547 — pancreatic repair not added (CPT covers the exclusion and gastrojejunostomy only); groups 423-425, not 405-407.
- 54125 — perineal urethrostomy not coded (no valid urethral Bypass value).
- 54390 — pediatric congenital repair; root operation and PDx choice (Q64.10 vs Q64.0) are judgment calls; low confidence.
- 54430 — corporal shunt coded as Drainage of penis (no penile Bypass); groups Penis Procedures.
- 55650 — PDx C63.7 (seminal vesicle neoplasm) is a guess; indication in Medicare patients is uncommon and varied.
- 55810/55812/55815 — 0VT00ZE ("capsule intact" qualifier) exists in FY2027; 0VT00ZZ used. Confirm which qualifier coders apply to radical prostatectomy.
