# Pass A, batch m16: codes for a second look

- 20962 / 20969 / 20970: assumes the recipient is the mandible (Replacement with autologous tissue plus the donor-bone Excision under B3.9). The recipient could be the maxilla or another bone; the oral-cancer diagnosis assumes head and neck reconstruction. These are add-on-type graft codes, so the real stay usually groups by the companion resection.
- 0202T: coded as Insertion of a facet replacement device (device value D). Decompression is treated as bed preparation and not coded; a reviewer may want a separate Release code (it would not change the family).
- 0219T / 0220T: intrafacet implants coded as posterior-column Fusion, percutaneous, with synthetic (J) or allograft (K) device. They are not interbody devices. 0220T (thoracic) is rare, and the diagnosis is a guess (low confidence).
- 0656T / 0657T: lumbar or thoracolumbar tether coded open (mini-open retroperitoneal). If the usual approach is endoscopic, swap to the 4-approach tether codes (0QS043Z / 0PS443Z). Medicare volume is essentially nil (pediatric scoliosis).
- 0790T: the Removal and Revision tables for vertebrae have no tether device value, so internal fixation (4) is used. Low confidence. The removal-and-replace variant groups differently (518-520 vs 495-497).
- 22010 / 22015: the muscle body part and side are a judgment call for a midline paraspinal abscess. The typical diagnosis (post-op deep wound infection) goes to MDC 18. The vertebral osteomyelitis alternative goes to soft-tissue procedures 500-502.
- 22206 / 22207 / 22210-22214 / 22220-22224: spinal osteotomies coded as Excision of the vertebra; the PCS index points "osteotomy" to Division. That choice may move the family between 515-517 and 518-520. In practice these are always done with a fusion (its own CPT), so the real stay groups to a fusion DRG, not to the osteotomy alone.
- 22220-22224: the discectomy is coded as Resection of the disc alongside the vertebral Excision. If the disc removal is partial, use Excision (same family).
- 22318: the nondisplaced variant (Insertion) groups to 515-517; the displaced Reposition groups to 518-520.
- 22319: the graft is coded as Supplement of the cervical vertebra. Check whether reviewers prefer leaving the graft uncoded.
- 22326: the cervical dislocation variant (Reposition of the vertebral joint) groups to 515-517, while the fracture variant groups to 518-520.
- 22532 / 22533: the lateral extracavitary approach uses fusion qualifier J. The typical diagnosis is metastasis (456-458), and fracture is the alternative (450/451). Which one is typical is a judgment call.
- 22586: presacral L5-S1 fusion coded percutaneous (3). Confirm that the approach and the interbody device value fit the axial rod.
- 22802: the long thoracic variant (8+ thoracic joints) groups to the extensive-fusion family 523-525, while the T10-S1 variant groups to 456-458. The family depends on which levels are fused.
