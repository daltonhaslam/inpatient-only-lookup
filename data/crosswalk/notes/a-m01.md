# Pass A, batch m01: codes for a second look

- 49000: procedure is clear (Inspection, peritoneal cavity), but the typical principal dx for a Medicare exploration with nothing else done is a judgment call. Unsalvageable bowel infarction chosen; carcinomatosis or trauma are plausible. All of these group to digestive or injury OR families.
- 49002: K91840 groups to 907-909 (injury/complication). If the re-exploration is a planned second look for ischemia, the dx would be K55.x and the family would be 356-358.
- 49013 / 49014: pelvic fracture with Control bleeding in the pelvic cavity groups to 981-983. That is how the grouper handles it without a multiple-trauma secondary (which would make it MDC 24, 957-959). The coding is correct as is; nothing was forced.
- 49014: there is no clear guidance on removing internal packing. Inspection, pelvic cavity is used here. Some coders use Removal of other device (0WPJ0YZ); packing may not count as a device in Med/Surg.
- 49062: body part for a lymphocele opened into the peritoneum. Pelvis lymphatic Drainage (079C0ZZ) is used here. Retroperitoneum (0W9H0ZZ) is the other reading, and it groups to 981-983 with I898, so it was dropped. A kidney-transplant complication dx (T86.1x) would change the family.
- 49186-49190: the principal dx decides the family. C786 gives 356-358. C480 (retroperitoneal sarcoma) gives 826-828. C480 is typical for 49189-49190 and an alternative for 49188. Organs resected en bloc are separate CPTs and are not coded here.
- 49215: low confidence. In adults the presacral space is coded as retroperitoneum. The dx is uncertain: benign soft-tissue tumor vs tailgut cyst vs chordoma (C41.4 would move it to MDC 8). In practice this is mostly a pediatric code.
- 49255: Excision (partial, infracolic) vs Resection (total) of omentum. Both are in the same family.
- 49425: the alternative uses C786 rather than R180, because R18.0 carries a code-first-malignancy note. Malignant ascites may now be the more common indication.
- 49428: low confidence on the root operation. Revision of the synthetic substitute (shunt ligated, device left in place) is used. Occlusion of the vein or Removal are the alternatives.
- 49605-49611: neonatal codes with essentially no Medicare volume. Mapped to Repair/Supplement of the abdominal wall.
- 49906: low confidence. Recipient site and dx vary widely: scalp, leg, chest wall, or gastroepiploic lymph node transfer for lymphedema (I89.0). Mapped as Replacement at the recipient plus Excision of omentum for the harvest (B3.9).
- 31290 / 31291: approach. Approach 8 is not valid for dura in FY2027 PCS, so percutaneous endoscopic (4) is used. Some guidance codes endonasal skull-base work as open. Also check whether the skull-base bone (ethmoid/sphenoid Supplement) is preferred over dura. If fat or fascia lata is used, a separate harvest code (0JB80ZZ / 0JBL0ZZ) would apply under B3.9.
- 31360 / 31365: the permanent tracheostoma (0B110Z4) is not coded separately. Confirm against Coding Clinic. It does not change the family (011-013 either way).
- 31365 / 31368: radical neck is coded as Resection of neck lymphatics plus Excision of the IJ vein, Resection of neck muscle and Excision of the accessory nerve (B3.2a). A reviewer may prefer coding the lymphatics only. The family is unchanged.
- 31370 / 31375 / 31380: Resection vs Excision for the epiglottis and vocal cords in partial laryngectomy depends on whether the whole structure is removed. All of these stay in 140-142.
