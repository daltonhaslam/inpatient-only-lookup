# Pass A, batch m20: codes for a second look

- 50100: the root operation depends on what happens to the vessel. Repositioning it (vascular hitch) is Reposition of the renal artery, which groups to 659-661. Dividing it is Occlusion, which groups to 673-675. Both are listed; confirm which is more common.
- 50120: exploration only is Inspection of the kidney (0TJ50ZZ). That code has no laterality and is rarely billed alone, and the principal dx (N2889) is a placeholder.
- 50125: assumed principal dx is pyonephrosis (N136). Obstructive hydronephrosis (N13x) groups to the same family.
- 50205: the open biopsy dx splits families. Medical renal disease (N179) groups to 659-661; a renal mass (D4101) groups to 656-658.
- 50220/50225/50546: the typical simple-nephrectomy dx is a judgment call. A nonfunctioning kidney (N261) groups to the non-neoplasm family; cancer (C641, listed as the alternative) groups to the neoplasm family.
- 50234/50236/50548/50650: the bladder-cuff Excision (0TBB0ZZ/0TBB4ZZ) moves these to Major Bladder Procedures 653-655 instead of kidney/ureter 656-658. This follows from coding the cuff as a separate body part; confirm the cuff is coded separately in practice.
- 50548: the bladder-cuff approach varies (open, endoscopic or laparoscopic). It is coded 0TBB4ZZ to match the laparoscopic CPT.
- 50290: a perinephric cyst is assigned to Retroperitoneum (0WBH0ZZ). Kidney (0TB0/0TB1) is the other option, depending on the body-part key. The dx is N281.
- 50400/50405: a dismembered pyeloplasty is coded as Excision of the kidney pelvis. A flap pyeloplasty is Repair. Both group to the same family.
- 50520/50525/50526: fistula closure is coded as Repair. The principal dx N2889 is a best fit. 50526 (chest approach) may need a diaphragm or pleura repair code.
- 50600: Drainage of the ureter vs Inspection; the dx is uncertain.
- 50605: open ureteral stent placement = Dilation of the ureter with an intraluminal device. This is non-OR in MS-DRG terms, so the case groups by diagnosis (medical 693/694).
- 50715: the RPF alternative (K682 as principal) groups to 981-983 unrelated. If the obstruction (N131) is sequenced first, it groups to 659-661. Sequencing needs a coder's call.
- 50728: ileal-conduit stoma revision = Repair of the ileum. Parastomal hernia repair is coded on the abdominal wall. With mesh (0WUF0JZ) and N99523, the case groups to 981-983 unrelated; without mesh, it groups to 659-661. Low confidence.
- 50740: Bypass from the kidney pelvis to the ureter is a best fit for ureteropyelostomy. Excision of the UPJ with reanastomosis is the other option.
