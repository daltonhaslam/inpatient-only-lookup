# Pass B, batch m16: codes for a second look

- 20962 / 20969 / 20970: free bone and osteocutaneous flaps are site-agnostic. I mapped them to jaw reconstruction (Replacement of mandible/maxilla with autologous tissue plus donor-bone Excision per B3.9). Supplement is the alternative if the bone augments rather than replaces a segment. Maxilla recipient sets group to 143-145, mandible sets to 140-142. Skin-paddle donor not coded separately.
- 0202T: Insertion of the facet-replacement stabilization device (0SH00DZ, from the device key) vs Replacement 0SR00JZ. I also added Release (00NY0ZZ) for the bundled decompression. Groups to 518 either way.
- 0219T / 0220T (low): intrafacet implant coded as posterior-column Fusion. Approach read as percutaneous (docked, image-guided). Device J vs K (allograft dowel) vs 7 is a judgment call. Interbody device isn't valid with the posterior-column qualifier. The XRG paired-cage facet codes are lumbar only, so they don't apply here.
- 0656T / 0657T: tether = Reposition with the tether device value, open approach assumed. Thoracoscopic thoracic portions would use 0PS443Z. Pediatric procedure, so Medicare volume is near zero.
- 0790T: the tether device value doesn't exist in the Revision/Removal tables, so I used internal fixation device. Revision and removal group to 495-497; replacement groups to 518-520.
- 22010 / 22015: the typical dx is a postop deep SSI (856-858). A primary paraspinal abscess (M6008) moves the case to 500-502. Body part neck vs trunk muscle; laterality is arbitrary for midline wounds; drain device (0) if a drain is left.
- 22206 / 22207 / 22210-22214: osteotomy wedge coded as Excision of the vertebra. Excision of the vertebral joint (posterior-column osteotomies) and Reposition are competing readings. The fusion is reported separately, so these group outside the fusion DRGs.
- 22220-22224: I added disc Resection alongside vertebral Excision (B3.2). This moves the family from 515-517 to 518-520.
- 22319: graft coded as Supplement of the cervical vertebra plus iliac donor Excision. Verify whether coders add the Supplement for a dens screw with graft.
- 22548: clivus-C2 coded as occipital-cervical plus cervical joint Fusion, anterior/open (the transoral incision counts as open). Device 7 assumed.
- 22556 / 22558: disc Resection added to the anterior interbody Fusion. The CPT frames the discectomy as interspace prep, so a reviewer may call it integral. No family change.
- 22586: presacral axial fusion. The qualifier (anterior approach, anterior column) and percutaneous approach are both judgment calls.
- 22802: set 3 (thoracic-only, 8+ joints) groups to extensive fusion 523-525. That case is more typical of adolescents than Medicare patients.
- 22804: whether a long fusion lands in 523-525 or 456-458 depends on the upper end level: 8+ thoracic joints (T4 or higher) vs 2-7.
