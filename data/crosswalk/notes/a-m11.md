# Pass A, batch m11: codes for a second look

- 27882: the qualifier (low vs mid) depends on where the guillotine cut is made. Staged guillotine cuts are often supramalleolar, so low is listed first. Every level groups to the same family.
- 27886: re-amputation is coded as Detachment at a higher lower-leg level. If only the bone end is trimmed, Excision of tibia/fibula is the alternative. The typical diagnosis (T8743, stump infection) groups to MS-DRG 474-476 (MDC 8), not to the circulatory amputation family.
- 27888: for an ankle (Syme) amputation, Detachment of the foot (complete) and Detachment of the lower leg (low) are both defensible. Both group the same way.
- 28800: for a midtarsal (Chopart) amputation, I used Detachment of the foot (complete). The complete qualifier is defined at the tarsometatarsal level, and no PCS value is more proximal than that. Worth confirming against Coding Clinic.
- 27882/27888/28800: E1152 groups to MDC 5 (239-241). The alternative, diabetic osteomyelitis (E1169), moves the case to 616-618.
- 47300: for marsupialization, I chose Excision (cyst wall removed) over Drainage. The PCS index allows either, and both group to 405-407.
- 47362: removing packing at a planned take-back has no clean root operation. I chose Inspection of the peritoneal cavity (0WJG0ZZ), which groups to 420-422. Extirpation (0WCG0ZZ) is the alternative. Low confidence.
- 47425/47460/47620: PCS has no Division value for the ampulla, so I coded the sphincterotomy or sphincteroplasty as Dilation of the ampulla (0F7C0ZZ). Repair and Drainage are the other candidates. Needs a coder check.
- 47605/47700: I added the operative-cholangiogram fluoroscopy code (BF101ZZ / BF131ZZ). Facility policy may omit it. It has no DRG effect.
- 47701: there is no PCS body part for the porta hepatis. I coded the Kasai as Resection of the CBD and gallbladder plus Bypass from the common hepatic duct to the jejunum. Low confidence. The case is pediatric, so Medicare volume is near zero.
- 47715: I included the hepaticojejunostomy reconstruction (0F170ZB) with Resection of the CBD. Whether the CPT code bundles the reconstruction needs checking. Either way, the DRG family does not change.
- 47711: for a bile duct tumor, Excision vs Resection of the CBD depends on whether the whole duct is removed. Both are listed, and both group to 408-410.
