# Pass B, batch m01: codes for a second look

- 49000: Inspection-only laparotomy; the principal dx (acute abdomen, R100) is a placeholder for a negative exploration. Real cases usually carry a definitive finding and a further procedure.
- 49002: Inspection vs Control bleeding depends on what the take-back finds. Both group to 907-909 with a postoperative-hemorrhage dx.
- 49010: Principal dx is uncertain (metastases vs lymphoma vs hematoma). A lymphoma dx with the diagnostic-excision set groups 981-983.
- 49013 / 49014: An isolated pelvic fracture (S32811A) with Control bleeding or Inspection of the pelvic cavity groups 981-983 (unrelated extensive). That is real MS-DRG logic for a single-injury case. Real patients are often multiple-trauma (MDC 24), which this single-dx grouper can't show. Packing removal has no PCS device value, so it was coded as Inspection, or as Control when the pelvis is repacked.
- 49062: Body part for the lymphocele (pelvic cavity vs retroperitoneum) and the dx (postprocedural I9789 vs kidney-transplant T8619) both call for judgment. Excision of peritoneum (the window) was rejected because it groups 987-989.
- 49186-49190: The body part depends on where the tumor sits. The dx changes with size: peritoneal metastases (C786 → 356-358) for small tumors, retroperitoneal sarcoma (C480 → 826-828) for 10 cm and up.
- 49215: The presacral space was coded as retroperitoneum. D367 groups 826-828. The body part and the histology are both soft.
- 49425: The qualifier is upper vein (venous limb to the jugular/SVC). Malignant ascites is sequenced to the malignancy (C786), because R180 says code first, and R180 alone groups 939-941.
- 49428: No PCS root operation clearly fits ligating a shunt. Revision of the synthetic substitute was chosen; Occlusion of the internal jugular (05LM0ZZ/05LN0ZZ) is the other reading.
- 49596, 49616-49618, 49621, 49622: These CPTs are "any approach", so open and laparoscopic sets are both included on purpose. The checker's approach-mismatch warning is expected here.
- 49605-49611: Neonatal codes with essentially no Medicare volume. They group to hernia DRGs 353-355 by dx.
- 49906: The free-flap recipient site (lower leg vs scalp) and the dx are guesses. Replacement vs Supplement at the recipient site is debatable.
- 31290 / 31291: Dura mater and skull-base bones have no natural-opening-endoscopic approach value. The endonasal approach was coded percutaneous endoscopic (4), against the brief's default of 8. Groups to 025-027.
- 31360 / 31365: The end tracheostoma was not coded separately (treated as closure). If coders add Bypass trachea to cutaneous (0B110Z4), the family is unchanged (011-013).
- 31365 / 31368: Radical neck dissection was coded by body part: neck lymphatics and neck muscle as Resection, internal jugular as Excision (PCS has no Resection value for the IJV). A coder might report the lymphatic code alone.
- 31367 / 31368 / 31370 / 31375 / 31380: Partial laryngectomies were split by body part (epiglottis or vocal cord Resection/Excision plus larynx Excision). They group to 140-142, not pre-MDC 011-013. A single-code Excision of larynx would group the same way.
