# Pass A, batch m19: codes for a second look

- 43500: two variants. Foreign-body removal (Extirpation, stomach) goes to 326-328. Exploration alone (Inspection, upper intestinal tract) goes to 356-358. Which variant is typical decides the family.
- 43620/43621/43622: Resection, stomach + Bypass esophagus to jejunum (0D150ZA). Confirm that the esophagojejunal reconstruction gets its own Bypass code here. The DRG is the same either way.
- 43631: Billroth I coded as Excision, stomach alone (no Bypass stomach to duodenum, because the anastomosis restores normal continuity). Some coders add 0D160Z9. The DRG is the same.
- 43640/43641/43800: pyloroplasty coded as Repair, pylorus (0DQ70ZZ). The index also offers Supplement. The principal dx (bleeding duodenal ulcer vs outlet obstruction vs gastroparesis) is a judgment call. All of these group to 326-328.
- 43645/43847: in a long-limb bypass, is the pouch joined to jejunum or ileum? Both are given as alternatives. Both group to 619-621.
- 43771: Revision, extraluminal device (0DW64CZ) with K9509 groups to 326-328, not the obesity family. Confirm that the band-complication dx is typical, versus E6601 when the revision is for inadequate weight loss.
- 43820: typical dx is pancreatic head cancer (palliative bypass), which gives 423-425. Benign outlet obstruction (K311) gives 326-328. The choice of typical dx changes the family.
- 43832: Bypass stomach to cutaneous is non-OR in MS-DRG terms, so the case groups medical (391/392) by the dysphagia dx. This is expected, but the dx (dysphagia vs head-and-neck or esophageal cancer) moves the medical DRG.
- 43840: Graham omental patch coded as Repair (pedicled omentum, so no Supplement/Transfer). Confirm.
- 43845: Duodenal switch = sleeve (0DB60Z3) + Bypass duodenum to ileum. The ileoileal anastomosis is not coded separately. Some coders add 0D1B0ZB.
- 43848: low confidence. Open bariatric revisions vary widely (conversion to RYGB, pouch resizing, ring removal). The dx K9589 sends every variant to 326-328. If the revision is for weight regain with E6601, the case would go to 619-621.
- 43860/43865: anastomosis revision coded as a redo Bypass stomach to jejunum. An Excision of the old anastomosis might also be reported.
- 43880: Repair of stomach + Repair of transverse colon. If a colon segment is resected, Excision, transverse colon would replace the colon Repair.
- 43881: the lead alone (0DH60MZ) with gastroparesis groups to 981-983 (unrelated OR). This is a grouper fact, not a coding error. Worth checking whether v44 assigns any gastric-stimulator code to MDC 6.
- 43882: Removal with K3184 gives 981-983, Revision with K3184 gives 326-328, and the typical infection dx (T8579XA) gives 907-909. The dx choice for lead complications is uncertain (T85.79 vs T85.1-/T85.73-).
- 50010/50045: low confidence. Open renal exploration/nephrotomy without other work is coded as Inspection, kidney. A vague typical dx (N2889); trauma (S37.0-) would move the MDC.
