# Pass A, batch m06: codes for a second look

- 58750: Repair vs Bypass (tube-to-same-tube values exist) vs Excision of the occluded segment for tubal reanastomosis. All three land in 742/743.
- 58760: Repair vs Release for fimbrioplasty. Same family either way.
- 58822: non-OR percutaneous drainage, so the case groups medical (757-759) by diagnosis. That is expected.
- 58825: the typical dx (cervical cancer before pelvic radiation) is a judgment call. Medicare volume is tiny.
- 58943/58950-58954: infracolic omentectomy coded as Excision of omentum (partial); Resection if total. Doesn't change the family.
- 58958: family depends on the dx. Recurrence coded as C78.6 (guideline I.C.2.d, primary site excised) gives 356-358; C56.9 gives 734/735. Low confidence.
- 58958/58960: pelvic lymphadenectomy as Resection of pelvic lymphatic (07TC0ZZ) drives 734/735. As Excision (07BC0ZZ) it gives 749/750. Coders split on this.
- 20661/20664: halo coded as Insertion of external fixation device into skull (percutaneous). It's non-OR, so the case is medical (551/552). The 6+ pin variant is indistinguishable in PCS.
- 21045: C03.1 (gum cancer invading bone) gives 140-142; C41.1 (primary mandible) gives 515-517. Both are listed.
- 21145-21147: one-, two- and three-piece LeFort I all collapse to the same PCS set. The autograft vs allograft choice is a guess.
- 21154/21155 and 21159/21160: the paired codes have identical PCS sets because an added LeFort I segment adds no body part. Orbit Reposition codes (0NSP04Z/0NSQ04Z) are arguable and left out.
- 21182-21184: Replacement (bone removed in the same session) chosen over Supplement. Graft area isn't captured in PCS.
- 21188: low confidence. Non-LeFort midface osteotomy covers many body parts, and the dx (M95.2) is a judgment call.
- 21247: Replacement of mandible (condyle) vs TMJ body parts. The rib donor is coded as 1-2 ribs.
- 21343/21344: S02.19 (frontal sinus fracture per the index) plus frontal bone Reposition groups to craniotomy 025-027. Surprising but follows from the grouper.
- 21431: closed reduction (external approach) is non-OR, so the case is medical (157-159). That is expected.
