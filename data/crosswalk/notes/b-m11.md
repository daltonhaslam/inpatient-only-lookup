# Pass B, batch m11: codes for a second look

- 27886: re-amputation coded as Detachment at the new lower-leg level (mid/high). A soft-tissue-only stump revision would be Excision of lower leg. T8753 (stump complication) groups 474-476, not the vascular family.
- 27888: Detachment foot "complete" vs lower leg "low". Both group 239-241 with E1152, so the DRG is the same either way, but reviewers may differ on the code.
- 28800: midtarsal level has no exact qualifier. Coded foot "complete". The ray qualifiers are the other reading. DRG is the same.
- 27882/27888/28800: E1152 groups to MDC 5 (239-241). The alternative E1169 (diabetic osteomyelitis) groups 616-618.
- 47122: two codes, Resection of one lobe plus Excision of the other. The alternative is a single Excision of liver (0FB00ZZ). DRG 405-407 either way.
- 47300: marsupialization coded as Excision of liver (unroofing). Drainage is the other index entry. Same family.
- 47361: debridement coded as Excision alone. Packing is not coded as Control under B3.7, and the suture repair is not coded separately. Some coders would add 0FQ00ZZ, which does not change the DRG.
- 47362: packing removal coded as Inspection of peritoneal cavity (0WJG0ZZ), which groups 420-422. Extirpation 0WCG0ZZ is the other reading and might group differently. Low confidence; Coding Clinic should be checked.
- 47425/47460/47620: the sphincter of Oddi has no Division value, so transduodenal sphincterotomy/sphincteroplasty is coded as Dilation of ampulla of Vater (0F7C0ZZ). With CBD stone clearance it is coded separately as a distinct objective. Check this against Coding Clinic.
- 47612/47620: the added biliary procedure moves the case from the cholecystectomy family into 408-410. I believe this is correct, but it should be confirmed.
- 47700/47701: neonatal procedures with no Medicare population. The Kasai body-part choice (Bypass, common hepatic duct to small intestine) and coding the resected remnant (CBD, gallbladder) separately are both judgment calls.
- 47711/47715: CPT may report reconstruction separately, but the PCS sets include the hepaticojejunostomy Bypass when it is done.
- 47712: intrahepatic duct tumor excision coded to the hepatic duct only. Any liver excision done with it would be coded in addition.
