# Pass B, batch m07: codes for a second look

- 21432/21433/21435/21436: coded as Reposition of maxilla plus both zygomas (one code per bone). Whether to also code nasal bone or orbit Reposition depends on which fracture lines are fixed. The family (140-142) doesn't change.
- 21436: graft coded as Supplement maxilla with autologous substitute plus donor-site Excision (calvarium or iliac crest, B3.9). The graft site could instead be the orbit (0NUP/0NUQ07Z). The family doesn't change.
- 31225/31230: PCS maxilla (R) is a single non-lateral body part, so a one-sided maxillectomy was coded as Excision, not Resection. Separate Resection of the maxillary sinus was added. I tested Resection 0NTR0ZZ and it gives the same family (143-145). Adding eye Resection for exenteration moves the case to 140-142.
- 31230: exenteration coded only as eye Resection (08T0XZZ/08T1XZZ, external is the only valid approach). Reviewers should decide whether orbital soft tissue or bony orbit needs its own code.
- 32658: the clot is in the pericardial space, so I used Extirpation of pericardial cavity (0WCD4ZZ) rather than pericardium (02CN4ZZ). Post-cardiac-surgery retained blood could take an I97.6- principal dx.
- 32659: I used Drainage of pericardial cavity, with no separate Excision of pericardium for the window. If tissue is sent as a specimen, some coders add 02BN4ZX. For a malignant effusion the cancer goes first, because I31.31 cannot be principal. With lung cancer as principal the case moves to 163-165.
- 44010: low confidence. The indication varies (stone or foreign body, biopsy, exploration), and each root operation groups to a different family: 326-328, 344-346 or 356-358.
- 44025: low confidence. A Medicare indication is uncommon and the typical dx is a guess. The Inspection-only variant groups to 356-358.
- 44050: sigmoid Reposition groups to 344-346, while small bowel and cecum go to 329-331. Small bowel volvulus or internal hernia is listed as typical.
- 44055: Ladd = Release of duodenum + Release of mesentery + appendectomy. It groups to adhesiolysis (335-337) unless a volvulus Reposition is added (329-331).
- 44127: excisional tapering stays inside the one jejunal Excision. Plication tapering would add Restriction (0DVA0ZZ). This is a neonatal code: the grouper's age logic (MDC 15) isn't reflected here.
- 44144: the mucous fistula is coded as a second Bypass to cutaneous (distal limb). For a sigmoid case the sigmoid becomes Excision because the distal stump stays. Confirm against coder practice.
- 44147: abdominal plus transanal approach coded as open, the most invasive approach used.
- 44155/44156: whether the anus is removed (0DTQ0ZZ) varies by technique. The family doesn't change.
- 44157/44158: the ileoanal connection is coded as Bypass ileum to anus (0D1B0ZQ). The alternative view treats it as restored continuity inside the Resection, with no code. The family doesn't change.
- 44188: a diverting colostomy for a stage 4 sacral pressure ulcer (alternative dx) groups to 981-983. That is the true MS-DRG behavior, not a mapping error.
