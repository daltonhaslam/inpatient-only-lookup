# Pass B, batch m17: codes for a second look

- 22808/22810/22812: device value. I used rib autograft (7) for thoracic anterior fusions and an interbody device (A) for thoracolumbar/lumbar. Adult degenerative scoliosis coded M41.8- (other forms), not M41.2-. For 22812, a thoracolumbar span groups to 456-458 but a pure 8+ thoracic set groups to "extensive" 523-525, so the body-part choice changes payment.
- 22818/22819: PCS has no Resection value for vertebrae, so I used Excision even though whole segments come out. The usual companion fusion is a separate CPT, so this family (515-517) understates the real stay. The diagnosis (congenital kyphosis) reflects a mostly pediatric procedure.
- 22830: an exploration alone is coded as Inspection. In practice it usually comes with hardware removal or a revision fusion billed under other CPTs.
- 22838: the family depends on the variant. Revision or removal groups to 495-497; removal plus a new tether groups to 518-520. I treated the tether as an "internal fixation device" in the Removal/Revision tables (device aggregation).
- 22849/22850/22852/22855: hardware is assigned to the vertebral-joint body part, not the vertebra. Revision or Removal+Insertion for 22849 is a judgment call. The diagnosis is a choice among T84.216A, T84.226A and T84.84XA (infection T84.7XXA is the other common one; same family).
- 22857/22862/22865: L5-S1 is coded as the lumbosacral disc (0SR4/0SW4/0SP4), which I listed first as the most common level.
- 22861/22862/22864/22865: the diagnosis is T84.098A (artificial disc treated as an "other internal joint prosthesis"). Coding Clinic support for that is unconfirmed.
- 41135/41145/41155/42426: a radical neck is coded as Resection of neck lymphatic plus Excision of SCM (neck muscle), IJV and accessory nerve (B3.2a distinct body parts). Another pass may code the lymphatic only. The family is the same either way.
- 41150/41153/41155: floor-of-mouth soft tissue has no specific PCS body part. I did not code it separately (0WB30ZZ is the only option). 41153 (tongue plus selective suprahyoid dissection) groups to 143-145, not major head and neck.
- 42845/42894: I treated the flap as its own CPT and left it out. Adding a pectoralis Transfer (0KXH0Z2) does not change the family (143-145).
- 42953: low confidence on both the target and the diagnosis (stricture vs fistula after laryngectomy). Coding it as Repair of pharynx with an esophageal diagnosis groups to 981-983, so I used upper esophagus.
- 43107/43108/43112/43113/43117/43118: I added Resection of the EGJ (0DT40ZZ) as a separate body part to each esophagectomy. The conduit is a Transfer to esophagus (stomach 0DX60Z5, colon 0DXE0Z5, small bowel 0DX80Z5). Neither choice changes the family.
- 43116: I used Replacement of esophagus with autologous tissue (the removal is inherent in Replacement), plus jejunum donor Excision (B3.9), with no separate Resection of the upper esophagus. ICD-10-CM has no cervical-esophagus code, so the diagnosis is C15.3.
