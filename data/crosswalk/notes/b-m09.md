# Pass B, batch m09: codes for a second look

- 45113: Excision vs Resection of rectum (mucosectomy leaves a muscular cuff), and whether the ileal pouch-anal join should be Bypass ileum to anus. The family does not change: the stoma/bypass codes put it in 329-331 either way.
- 45119 / 45397: the diverting loop ileostomy (Bypass ileum to cutaneous) moves the case from 332-334 to 329-331. Most of these cases get a diverting stoma, so the set with it is listed first. Sigmoid resection alongside the rectum is not coded.
- 45121: the ileoanal pull-through after total colectomy is coded as Bypass ileum to anus. The subtotal set codes each colon segment removed.
- 45123: Excision is assumed (partial rectum). The diagnosis is uncertain for a perineal resection without reconnection.
- 45126: multi-organ sets. The ileal conduit is coded as Bypass ureters to ileocutaneous plus Excision ileum for the conduit (B3.9). Prostate and uterus/cervix vary by sex. The family is 329-331 for all variants.
- 45130 / 45135: Excision vs Resection of rectum for perineal or abdominoperineal prolapse resection. Adding sigmoid excision moves the case from 332-334 to 329-331.
- 45136: pouch excision is coded as Excision ileum. The diagnosis K91858 is a judgment call; pouchitis (K91850) or Crohn disease are alternatives.
- 45395: mixed approach. Rectum and stoma are coded laparoscopic (4) and the anus is coded open (0) for the perineal phase.
- 45400 / 45402 / 45540 / 45550: rectopexy is coded as Reposition rectum. Mesh rectopexy could arguably add Supplement.
- 45562 / 45563: the presacral drain is not coded. Trauma laceration is the assumed diagnosis; in Medicare patients an iatrogenic perforation is plausible.
- 45800 / 45805 / 45820 / 45825: the fistula closure is coded as Repair on both organs. A urinary-system principal diagnosis puts these in MDC 11 (653-655 or 662-664). If the principal diagnosis were the underlying cause (e.g. rectal cancer), they would group to MDC 6.
- 46705 / 46715: Repair anus vs Dilation of anus (cutback/stricture incision). Low confidence on 46715.
- 46716: Reposition anus for fistula transposition.
- 46730-46742: the pull-through is coded as Reposition rectum. Fistula closure is coded as Repair urethra or vagina.
- 46744 / 46746 / 46748: cloacal repair has a multi-component construction and low confidence. 46748 adds Supplement vagina with a harvested bowel segment; the source bowel segment (sigmoid vs ileum) varies.
- 46751: R159 is an assumed diagnosis for pediatric sphincteroplasty.
- 27365: the diagnosis assumes metastasis (C7951); primary sarcoma (C4021) is an alternative. Endoprosthetic reconstruction is not included.
- 27448: osteotomy without fixation is coded as Reposition with no device (Division is the alternative). The diagnosis M21061 is a guess.
