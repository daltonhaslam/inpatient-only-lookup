# Pass B, batch m15: codes for a second look

- 19305 / 19306: pectoral muscle Resection (0KTH/0KTJ) plus breast and nodal Resection groups to 579-581, not 582-583. Without the muscle code it would group as mastectomy for malignancy. Is a separate muscle code right for Halsted/Urban? Urban's en bloc chest-wall segment is not coded.
- 23200 / 23210: whether a radical bone-tumor resection is Excision (partial bone) or Resection (whole clavicle/scapula). Both are listed; same family.
- 23220: proximal humerus treated as the Humeral Head body part (Resection) plus Excision of shaft when the cut extends distally. Same family either way.
- 24931: the implant has no Detachment device value, so only the amputation is coded. Low confidence.
- 24940 (cineplasty) and 25915 (Krukenberg): no PCS construct names these; coded to Transfer of a skin/subq tube and Division of forearm muscle. With Z89 dx they group to 939-941. Low confidence.
- 25920: wrist disarticulation coded Detachment hand, complete. The other defensible choice is lower arm, low. Same family.
- 25927: transmetacarpal coded as partial-ray Detachment for all five rays (complete hand = CMC level). Check whether a coder would report 5 codes or 1.
- 24900 / 24931 / 25900 / 25920 / 25927: typical dx is a judgment call (arm ischemic gangrene vs necrotizing infection vs tumor vs trauma); the alternative shifts 255-257 to 474-476.
- 26551 / 26553 / 26554: Z89 (delayed reconstruction) groups to 939-941. If done acutely (S68 dx) it groups to 906. Finger-target toe transfer has no toe-qualified code, so it is coded as finger phalanx Replacement plus donor toe Detachment.
- 26556 / 20957: the donor-site code (toe joint / metatarsal Excision, B3.9) drives the DRG to Foot Procedures 503-505.
- 15756-15758: free flaps coded as Replacement of recipient subq/fascia (0JR) plus donor Excision rather than Transfer. The recipient body part and typical dx (lower-leg osteomyelitis) are assumptions. 15757/15758 group 463-465 and 15756 groups 500-502 because the donor is muscle.
- 15778: changed from 0WUF0JZ (grouped unrelated 987-989 with M726) to 0JU80JZ subq/fascia Supplement. Confirm the body-part choice.
- 11004 / 11006: female variants (vulva Excision) dropped because N493 is male-only and dx is not per-set. Female necrotizing perineal infection would group differently.
- 19361-19369: pedicled flaps coded as Transfer (0KX with flap qualifier) and free flaps as Replacement of breast (0HR with flap qualifier, no separate donor code). Typical dx Z421 (584-585); immediate reconstruction with C50 groups 582-583.
