# Pass A, batch m10: codes for a second look

- 27725 (low): no PCS root operation fits a tibiofibular synostosis. Coded as Reposition tibia with fixation + Supplement tibia with autologous bone. Same family (492-494) under any reasonable reading.
- 27727 (low): congenital tibial pseudarthrosis repair coded as Reposition tibia with IM device + Supplement with autograft. Q742 is a nonspecific dx (no specific ICD-10-CM code for this). Family 492-494.
- 27466 / 27715 (medium): lengthening coded as osteotomy (Division) + Insertion of limb-lengthening device. Without the Division code, the external-fixator variant (0QH808Z/0QHG08Z) is non-OR and groups medical (564-566). Confirm that a coder would report Division separately from the lengthening device.
- 27465 / 27715 shortening (medium): Excision of a femoral or tibial segment + Reposition with fixation. In the v44 hierarchy the femur Excision row (498-499) ranks above 480-482, so 27465 groups to 498-499.
- 27486 / 27487 / 27580 (spacer-removal sets): v44 has no revision family (466-468). With a non-infection dx, knee component Removal codes group to 463-465 under the v44 tables, ahead of 469-470. That looks surprising but is how the v44 table and hierarchy work. Worth a reviewer's eye.
- 27580 (medium): typical dx assumed to be failed infected TKA (T8453XA). Fusion alone groups to 488-489 because the 400 row doesn't list this dx. Adding spacer removal moves the case to 463-465.
- 27558 (medium): ligament reconstruction coded as Replacement of knee bursa and ligament. Supplement is the other reading; both group to 488-489.
- 27645 / 27646 (medium): radical tumor resection coded as Excision (partial bone). Resection sets are listed for whole-bone removal. Both group to 492-494.
- 27450 / 27470 (medium): typical dx is a femur malunion/nonunion 7th-character code. Body part is femoral shaft vs lower femur, depending on the site.
- 27596 (medium): re-amputation coded as Detachment at the new (higher) level. The dx is an infected stump (T8743), which groups to 474-476, not the circulatory amputation family.
