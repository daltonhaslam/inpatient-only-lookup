# Pass B, batch m08: codes for a second look

- 44205: right colectomy coded as Resection right large intestine + Excision ileum (terminal ileum taken). Ileocecal valve not coded separately; some coders add it. Same family either way.
- 44210 / 44211 / 44212: "Large Intestine" (E) treated as excluding the rectum (rectum = separate body part P). Ileorectal and ileal-pouch-anal anastomoses treated as integral to the resection; 0D1B4ZP / 0D1B4ZQ exist and could be argued. Family unchanged.
- 44227 / 44626: Hartmann reversal coded as Bypass colon to rectum (0D1M?ZP). Excision of the stoma end or Reposition of the colon are the competing readings. All stay in 329-331.
- 44620 / 44625 / 44227: stoma takedown principal dx Z432 (ileostomy) used as typical. Use Z433 for colostomy takedowns. Same family.
- 44300: a feeding-device insertion is non-OR in MS-DRG terms and groups medically (374-376 with esophageal cancer). The decompressive cecostomy variant groups 344-346.
- 44314 / 44345: extensive stoma revision, Repair vs Excision of the stoma segment. Both 329-331.
- 44320: added an alternative dx, stage 4 sacral pressure ulcer (diverting colostomy). It groups to 981-983 (unrelated OR). We expect this: MDC 9 has no colostomy in its OR list.
- 44346: if the stoma is relocated (Reposition), the case groups 344-346 instead of 329-331. Whether the colostomy revision should get a code next to the abdominal-wall repair is a judgment call.
- 44660 / 44661: typical dx is diverticulitis (MDC 6). The alternative, fistula N321 as principal, moves the case to 653-655 (major bladder). The choice of principal dx is debated.
- 44680: plication/fixation of bowel loops coded as Reposition of small intestine. No clean PCS root. Low confidence.
- 44700: pelvic bowel-exclusion sling. Mesh = Supplement peritoneum (356-358). Omental sling = Transfer omentum to pelvis, which the grouper puts in hernia procedures (350-352). Low confidence.
- 44820: typical dx (peritoneal metastasis) is a guess. The lesions vary (desmoid, carcinoid, cyst, lymphoma), but every option stays in MDC 6.
- 44899: unlisted code, marked unmappable.
- 45111 / 45112: the rectum-only variant groups to Rectal Resection 332-334. The variant that adds the sigmoid groups to 329-331.
- 45110: APR = rectum + anus Resection + end-colostomy Bypass. The anal sphincter (0DTR) is not coded separately.
