# Pass B, batch m10: codes for a second look

- 27486 / 27487: knee arthroplasty revision coded the standard way (Removal 0SP + Replacement 0SR; insert exchange = Removal of liner + Supplement with liner). The grouper sends these to 463-465 (Wound Debridement/Skin Graft), not 449 (Revision of Hip or Knee Prosthesis). In the v44 Appendix E that the grouper reads, only 0SW (Revision root operation) codes carry a 449 row; 0SP removal codes carry 403-404 and 463-465 rows, and 463-465 ranks above 469-470 in the MDC 08 hierarchy. Check this against the real v44 grouper before publishing the payment for these two codes. If CMS's logic is pair-based, the family here is a tool artifact; it is not a reason to recode as Revision (0SW).
- 27450: Reposition with fixation vs Division (the PCS index default for osteotomy). The family is the same either way (480-482).
- 27465 / 27466 / 27715: limb shortening coded as Excision + Insertion of fixation, and lengthening as Division + Insertion of the limb-lengthening device. Reposition with device is the competing view for shortening. Coding Clinic guidance on lengthening-nail cases should be confirmed.
- 27470 / 27472 / 27724: nonunion takedown coded as Reposition with fixation, plus Supplement with graft and iliac harvest (B3.9) when grafted. An Excision + Insertion reading is possible but stays in the same family.
- 27495: CPT does not fix the approach. I used percutaneous intramedullary nailing, the dominant technique for impending pathologic fracture. Open plating (0QH804Z) groups to the same family.
- 27506: open approach per the CPT. Most femoral nailing is a closed reduction, and a coder would report it as percutaneous (0QS836Z) when the fracture site is not opened. The family is the same.
- 27558: the ligament reconstruction is coded as Replacement (0MRN0KZ, allograft). Use Supplement (0MUN0KZ) when the native ligament is left and augmented. Same family.
- 27580: the typical diagnosis is an infected knee prosthesis. Fusion alone groups to 488-489 because 0SGC has no 403-404 row. In practice the case is often billed together with prosthesis removal (27488), which would move the claim to 403-404.
- 27596: re-amputation is coded as Detachment at the more proximal level. A soft-tissue-only stump revision would be Excision, which groups differently.
- 27645 / 27646: radical tumor resection coded as Excision. Resection applies only if the entire bone is removed.
- 27725 (low confidence): tibiofibular synostosis coded as Reposition of fibula with fixation + Supplement tibia with autograft + harvest. No clean PCS analog exists.
- 27727: congenital pseudarthrosis coded as Reposition with IM rod + Supplement with graft + harvest. The excision of hamartomatous tissue is treated as bone-end preparation and not coded separately. A reviewer may want 0QBG0ZZ added. Same family.
- 27455: the diagnosis (Blount disease) reflects the clinical indication. A Medicare inpatient with open growth plates is essentially nonexistent.
