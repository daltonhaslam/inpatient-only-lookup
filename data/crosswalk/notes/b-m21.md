# Pass B, batch m21: codes for a second look

- 50750: PCS bypass has no calyx or kidney value, so I used Bypass kidney pelvis to ureter as the closest code. Lower-pole amputation counts as the access step and isn't coded.
- 50760: I chose Excision of the strictured segment because the urine route doesn't change. A pure reanastomosis with no segment removed would be Reposition, and some coders would pick Bypass ureter to ureter. All three group to the same family.
- 50780 / 50782 / 50783: Bypass vs Reposition depends on the indication. Adult distal stricture: Bypass ureter to bladder. Reflux, duplex or megaureter reimplant with the route unchanged: Reposition. Both group to the same family. 50783 adds Excision for the tapering as a distinct objective; this is a judgment call.
- 50785: The psoas hitch adds Reposition bladder, which moves the case from Kidney/Ureter non-neoplasm (659-661) to Major Bladder (653-655). Boari-flap-only sets stay in 659-661. The guidelines support both families, so check which variant is typical.
- 50810 / 50815 / 50820 / 50825 / 50860: The diagnosis drives the family. Bladder cancer groups to 656-658 and non-neoplasm to 659-661, so I gave each an alternative dx. Standalone diversions in Medicare are often cancer-related or done with an exenteration billed elsewhere. 50810 is rare, so it is low confidence.
- 50815 / 50820 / 50825 / 50840 / 50845 / 51590 / 51595 / 51596 / 51597: Bowel or appendix donor segment coded per B3.9 (Excision ileum/colon, Resection appendix/cecum). The grouping doesn't change, but a reviewer may prefer the Bypass code alone.
- 50830: Undiversion takes many forms, so this is low confidence. Z436 as principal is a judgment call; a stoma complication from N99.5- is the alternative.
- 50930: Bowel partner and repair-vs-resection vary, so this is low confidence.
- 50940: Release vs Extirpation of the suture is a judgment call. N9989 as principal is a judgment call too.
- 51575 / 51585 / 51595 / 51596 / 51597: Male sets add Resection of the prostate and seminal vesicles. The family doesn't change.
- 51596: I coded the orthotopic neobladder as Replacement of bladder with autologous tissue, with the bladder removal included in Replacement and no separate Resection. Confirm with reviewers.
- 51800: A Y-V plasty coded as Dilation of bladder neck is non-OR and groups medical (698-700). The wedge-resection and fundus-resection variants group to Major Bladder. Low confidence; Repair bladder neck is the other candidate.
- 51820: I chose Restriction of bladder neck (tubularization) plus Reposition of both ureters; Repair is the alternative, with the same family. Low confidence, and the indication is mostly pediatric.
- 51900 / 51920: The female fistula dx moves these to MDC 13 (746-747 / 742-743). That is expected for an N82- principal.
- Hip/knee grouper fix: not relevant to this urinary batch.
