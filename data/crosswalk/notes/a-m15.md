# Pass A, batch m15: codes for a second look

- 24940: there is no PCS concept for a skin-lined muscle tunnel. Mapped to Transfer, upper arm muscle with skin (0KX70Z0/0KX80Z0) with Z89221, which groups to 939-941. Low confidence.
- 25915: the same problem applies to splitting a forearm stump into two rami. Mapped to Division, lower arm and wrist muscle (0K890ZZ/0K8B0ZZ) with Z89211, which groups to 939-941. Low confidence.
- 24931: PCS has no device value for an implant at a transhumeral amputation. Mapped to Detachment plus Insertion of an internal fixation device into the humeral shaft (0PHF04Z/0PHG04Z). The Detachment code drives the DRG.
- 24930, 25924: a stump re-amputation is coded as Detachment at the new, more proximal level. If a reviewer reads it as Excision of the humeral shaft or radius/ulna, the family moves out of the amputation family.
- 25927: amputation through the metacarpal shafts is coded as five "partial ray" Detachment qualifiers (complete = through the CMC joints). Some coders use a single 0X6J0Z0 instead. The DRG family is the same either way.
- 26551, 26553: the toe-to-thumb qualifier N/P already captures the toe donor, the way DIEP/TRAM breast qualifiers capture theirs, so no separate code for the toe harvest. The iliac graft for the wraparound is coded separately (B3.9). Z89011 as principal dx groups to 939-941, and the acute S68011A groups to 906. Check whether Z89 is acceptable as principal for a delayed reconstruction.
- 26553: a lesser toe can go to a finger, and fingers have no Replacement-with-toe value. Only the thumb recipient was mapped.
- 26554: low confidence. Finger recipients are coded as Supplement with autologous tissue plus Detachment of the donor toe (B3.9). No Coding Clinic guidance was found for toe-to-finger.
- 26556: B3.9 donor excision of the toe joint (0SBP/0SBQ/0SBM) outranks the hand procedure in the MDC 8 hierarchy, so the case groups to Foot Procedures 503-505. That is technically correct, but confirm that a coder would report the donor joint.
- 15756, 15757, 15758: free flaps to non-breast sites are coded as Replacement of the recipient subcutaneous tissue and fascia with autologous tissue, plus Excision of the donor site (B3.9). The recipient site (lower leg/foot) and the donor body part are assumptions, because the CPT doesn't name either one. The family moves with the donor code and the dx: 500-502, 463-465, 579-581 or 570-572. Low confidence.
- 15778: mesh across a debrided fascial defect is coded as Supplement, subcutaneous tissue and fascia (0JU80JZ/0JUB0JZ) with M726, which groups to 500-502. Coding it 0WUF0JZ (abdominal wall) with M726 or N493 groups to 987-989 (unrelated). In practice this code is usually billed alongside 11004-11006 debridement, and the debridement drives the DRG.
- 19306: an Urban procedure that takes chest wall en bloc might also carry rib/sternum Excision codes. Those were left out because they are part of the same resection.
- 19361-19369: Z421 (delayed) groups to 584-585. Immediate reconstruction with C50911 groups to 582-583, listed as the alternative. A breast implant placed with a latissimus flap was not coded.
- 20955-20957: the mandible is assumed to be the recipient, with an oral-cavity cancer dx (140-142). A long-bone or hand recipient would change the family. The 20957 metatarsal graft is rare in Medicare. Low confidence.
- 23200, 23210, 23220: whether the principal dx is primary bone sarcoma or a bone metastasis (C7951) doesn't change the family (515-517 / 492-494).
